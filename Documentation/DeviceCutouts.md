# Device display cutouts

Verified against Apple's published model descriptions and product pages on September 25, 2026.

Built-in bezels use the display cutout type of the selected model. Their shapes are original artwork: widths, heights, corner curves, and positions are approximations, not official Apple measurements.

| Display cutout | Models in the catalog |
| --- | --- |
| Wider notch attached to the top edge | iPhone 12, iPhone 12 mini, iPhone 12 Pro, iPhone 12 Pro Max |
| Smaller notch attached to the top edge | iPhone 13, iPhone 13 mini, iPhone 13 Pro, iPhone 13 Pro Max; iPhone 14, iPhone 14 Plus; iPhone 16e; iPhone 17e |
| Dynamic Island | iPhone 14 Pro, iPhone 14 Pro Max; all iPhone 15 models; iPhone 16, iPhone 16 Plus, iPhone 16 Pro, iPhone 16 Pro Max; iPhone 17, iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max; iPhone Air |
| Smaller Dynamic Island | iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max |
| No display cutout | iPhone SE (3rd generation); all iPads in the catalog |

## Apple sources

- [Identify your iPhone model](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108044) includes front views and identifies the models with Dynamic Island. Its iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max descriptions explicitly identify a smaller Dynamic Island. Its iPhone SE description identifies the Home button and 4.7-inch display.
- [Apple introduces iPhone 13 and iPhone 13 mini](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2021/09/apple-introduces-iphone-13-and-iphone-13-mini/) describes a redesigned, smaller TrueDepth camera system compared with the preceding generation.
- [Apple unveils iPhone 13 Pro and iPhone 13 Pro Max](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2021/09/apple-unveils-iphone-13-pro-and-iphone-13-pro-max-more-pro-than-ever-before/) describes a 20 percent smaller **area** for the TrueDepth system. This does not establish an exact reduction in notch width or provide cutout pixel dimensions.
- [Apple debuts iPhone 14 Pro and iPhone 14 Pro Max](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2022/09/apple-debuts-iphone-14-pro-and-iphone-14-pro-max/) introduces Dynamic Island for the Pro models. The standard iPhone 14 and iPhone 14 Plus retain the attached notch shown in Apple's model identification page.
- [Apple debuts iPhone 15 and iPhone 15 Plus](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2023/09/apple-debuts-iphone-15-and-iphone-15-plus/) confirms Dynamic Island on both standard models.
- [iPhone 16e technical specifications](https://support.apple.com/en-us/122208) show the notched front and list the 6.1-inch display. [Apple's iPhone 16e accessories page](https://www.apple.com/shop/accessories/all/cases-protection/iphone-16e) also describes a screen protector shared with iPhone 14, iPhone 13, and iPhone 13 Pro, including its notch cutout.
- [Shop iPhone 17e](https://www.apple.com/us/shop/buy-iphone/iphone-17e) explicitly describes the front image as having a notch with the TrueDepth camera. Its [technical specifications](https://www.apple.com/iphone-17e/specs/) do not list Dynamic Island.
- [iPhone Air technical specifications](https://www.apple.com/iphone-air/specs/) and [iPhone 18 Pro technical specifications](https://www.apple.com/iphone-18-pro/specs/) list Dynamic Island. The smaller size of the iPhone 18 Pro family's island is established by the model identification page above, not by an official pixel measurement.

## Mirrored screen content

Selecting a bezel changes the frame and its cutout overlay; it does not change the connected device's iOS interface. Mirroring preserves the captured screen pixels, so a source device's Live Activities or other Dynamic Island content remain in the feed regardless of the selected bezel. A different bezel can cover part of that content but cannot turn it into the status-bar layout of another model.
